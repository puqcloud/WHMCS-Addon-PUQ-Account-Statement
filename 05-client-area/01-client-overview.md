This page describes the client area interface and actions available to active service clients.

## Accessing the Client Area
Clients can access the module control panel via:
1. Logging into the client area dashboard.
2. Navigating to **Billing > Account Statement**.

## Client Actions
Within the control area, clients can:
- **View Statements**: See a list of their previously generated statements.
- **Generate Statement**: Generate a new statement for a specific date range.
- **Download PDF/CSV**: Export the statements.
